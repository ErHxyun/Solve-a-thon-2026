# Blind test: score 20 federal grant opportunities for NC DNCR

You are a grant strategist for the **North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources (DNCR)**: state parks, historic sites and the State Historic Preservation Office, state museums, the State Archives, the State Library, the NC Arts Council and the Natural Heritage Program. DNCR has one grant-development staff member with about 10 hours a week for grants. Its 2025–2029 Strategic Plan centres on education, stewardship of natural and cultural resources, resilience, and sites as community anchors.

Below are 20 opportunities. **Some DNCR has won in the past and some are not a fit; you are not told which.** Do not search for whether DNCR or North Carolina received any of them; judge only from the information given plus general knowledge of each program.

## Use exactly your earlier rules

Score each criterion 1–5 (whole numbers):

| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic Alignment (A) | 0.35 | no clear link to DNCR goals | supports one goal indirectly | directly advances multiple DNCR objectives |
| Funding Value (V) | 0.20 | < $50k or no direct cash | $50k–$250k | > $250k or multiple awards |
| Eligibility & Feasibility (E) | 0.20 | DNCR ineligible, or match/partnership unrealistic | eligible but complex | DNCR clearly eligible as a state agency, no onerous match |
| Workload (W) | 0.15 | > 200 staff hours | 80–150 hours | < 80 hours |
| Urgency (U) | 0.10 | deadline > 12 months away | 3–6 months | < 3 months |

Final = 0.35A + 0.20V + 0.20E + 0.15W + 0.10U. Today is 2026-11-07. If a deadline is not posted, score U = 3.

## Output

1. One table, sorted by Final score (highest first): ID, title, A, V, E, W, U, Final, and a one-line reason.
2. For every item, a verdict: **Pursue**, **Consider**, or **Skip**.
3. Do not change the rules or the weights, and do not leave any item unscored.
