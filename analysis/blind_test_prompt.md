DNCR has one grant-development staff member with about 10 hours a week for grants. Its 2025–2029 Strategic Plan centres on education, stewardship of natural and cultural resources, resilience, and sites as community anchors.

Below are 40 opportunities. **Some DNCR has won in the past and some are not a fit; you are not told which.** Do not search for whether DNCR or North Carolina received any of them; judge only from the information given plus general knowledge of each program.

## Use exactly your earlier rules

Score each criterion 1–5 (whole numbers):

| Criterion | Weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic Alignment (A) | 0.35 | no clear link to DNCR goals | supports one goal indirectly | directly advances multiple DNCR objectives |
| Funding Value (V) | 0.20 | < $50k or no direct cash | $50k–$250k | > $250k or multiple awards |
| Eligibility & Feasibility (E) | 0.20 | DNCR ineligible, or match/partnership unrealistic | eligible but complex | DNCR clearly eligible as a state agency, no onerous match |
| Workload (W) | 0.15 | > 200 staff hours | 80–150 hours | < 80 hours |
| Urgency (U) | 0.10 | deadline > 12 months away | 3–6 months | < 3 months |

Final = 0.35A + 0.20V + 0.20E + 0.15W + 0.10U. Today is 2026-11-07. To keep timing neutral, every item has the same deadline (2027-04-05); score U the same way for all of them. Estimate workload hours yourself from the description.

## Output

1. One table, sorted by Final score (highest first): ID, title, A, V, E, W, U, Final, and a one-line reason.
2. For every item, a verdict: **Pursue**, **Consider**, or **Skip**.
3. Do not change the rules or the weights, and do not leave any item unscored.

- Eligible applicants: state governments
- Deadline: 2027-04-05
- Award: ceiling $7,094,211; 14 expected awards
- Description: The 811 PRA program promotes the development of innovative state-level strategies to increase access to new and existing affordable supportive housing units for persons with disabilities. The program also supports collaborations between State Housing Agencies and State Health and Human Service/Medicaid Agencies to increase access to supportive services. Many states have already formed partnerships to address this need, and HUD intends to further support these efforts while incentivizing other states to take similar approaches. This program gives states the flexibility to award and administer these funds to address the shortage of affordable and integrated housing for persons with disabilities. Housing agencies may either directly administer the rental assistance contracts for eligible properties or contract with other qualified parties to administer this assistance.&nbsp;

### G39. BJA FY 2026 Emmett Till Unsolved Civil Rights Crimes Act Program
- Agency: Bureau of Justice Assistance
- Eligible applicants: other
- Deadline: 2027-04-05
- Award: ceiling $645,000
- Description: This is a notice of funding opportunity (NOFO) for the BJA FY 2026 Emmett Till Unsolved Civil Rights Crimes Act Program. This opportunity supports state, local, and tribal law enforcement agencies, including prosecution agencies, in investigating and prosecuting criminal offenses involving civil rights that occurred no later than December 31,1979, and resulted in a death.

### G40. DARRP Restoration Implementation Grants (2026)
- Agency: DOC NOAA - ERA Production
- Eligible applicants: other
- Deadline: 2027-04-05
- Award: ceiling $100,000,000
- Description: The principal objective of the Damage Assessment, Remediation and Restoration Program (DARRP) Restoration Implementation Grants is for NOAA to collaborate with non-federal partners to implement marine and coastal habitat restoration and conservation in order to accomplish common goals related to oil spills, hazardous substance releases, and other natural resource damages. Successful applicants will support and implement restoration activities authorized under the Oil Pollution Act, Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act, Clean Water Act, National Marine Sanctuaries Act, and other federal agencies' authorities related to oil spills, hazardous substance releases, and natural resource damages, such as the RESTORE Act. Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) will be for those capable of implementing restoration and conservation activities across a wide geographic scale.
